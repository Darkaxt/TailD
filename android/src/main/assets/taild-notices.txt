Windows world-50m.json country topology from Safing Portmaster commit
4e59231aa551697abd4ce85ed1ec7720fac2c3d2, SHA-256
c8598aca2a616f5fcdda15a95c5ee2d7c2be4ecc30edf67943c79e5ae97ee6e5.
Retain its upstream GPL-3.0 distribution/source notice; license text is bundled
as taild-map-license.txt. Original country geography is Natural Earth data,
public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/ .
Made with Natural Earth. Static boundaries do not represent network routes or
device location. Reproduce the projected asset with scripts/taild/android_country_map.mjs
and the pinned desktop topology. Build-time d3-geo 3.1.1 and topojson-client 3.1.0
are ISC-licensed; no JavaScript library, remote map service or copied Portmaster
renderer code is included in the Android runtime.
